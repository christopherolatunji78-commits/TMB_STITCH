About Us

MISS TALABI MARY BOLUWATIFE

TMB STITCH TMB Stitches is a fashion and beauty brand *crafted with patience and designed with purpose*. The name *TMB* comes from our founder *Talabi Mary Boluwatife*,
a name she has been known for since her secondary school days. With *three years of dedicated training* rooted in patience and respect for the craft, we specialize in *custom stitching,
fashion design, hair accessories, and beauty essentials like lip care products*. At TMB Stitches, we believe every stitch tells a story and every detail matters.
